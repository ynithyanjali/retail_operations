# Retail Operations & Business Analysis

### Business Performance | SQL | Excel | Tableau

## Project Overview

A portfolio case study examining the operational and financial performance of a fictional retail business.

The project explores how business analysts can use data to identify operational inefficiencies, evaluate key performance indicators, document business requirements, and recommend measurable improvements.

**Project Type:** Business Analysis & Data Analytics  
**Data:** Synthetic, fictional retail transactions  
**Status:** In Development

## Business Problem

A fictional retail company is experiencing several operational challenges:

- Increasing product return rates
- Uneven sales performance across product categories
- Delayed order fulfillment
- Declining profitability in selected business segments

The objective is to investigate these problems, identify their potential drivers, and develop evidence-based recommendations.

## Project Objectives

1. Analyze revenue, profitability, and customer purchasing trends.
2. Evaluate product returns and delivery performance.
3. Identify operational inefficiencies through KPI analysis.
4. Document business requirements and process improvements.
5. Present findings through an executive dashboard.

## Planned Tools & Technologies

- **SQL (MySQL):** Data querying and analysis
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning and KPI calculations
- **Tableau:** Business dashboard and visualization
- **GitHub:** Project documentation and version control

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Monitor sales performance |
| Profit Margin | Evaluate profitability |
| Return Rate | Identify product and customer experience issues |
| On-Time Delivery Rate | Measure fulfillment efficiency |
| Average Order Value | Understand customer spending patterns |

## Planned Deliverables

- Synthetic retail dataset
- SQL analysis queries
- Executive KPI dashboard
- Business Requirements Document (BRD)
- Business process maps
- User stories and acceptance criteria
- Findings and recommendations report

## Methodology

**Define Business Problems → Prepare Data → Analyze KPIs → Identify Root Causes → Document Requirements → Recommend Improvements**

## Project Status

This project is being developed as a portfolio case study. Findings, dashboard outputs, and recommendations will be added after the corresponding analyses are completed.

All business scenarios and transaction records are fictional and intended for educational and portfolio purposes.
