Retail Performance Analysis

A portfolio project where I played business analyst for a made-up retail company.

I wanted to practice the full loop: start with a vague business problem, dig into the data with SQL, and end up with something an executive could act on. The data is synthetic (1,200 orders across 4 US regions), so the company and every number in it are fictional.

The problem

The company’s returns were creeping up, some categories were selling much better than others, orders were shipping late, and profit was slipping in a few areas. I wanted to find out where and why.

What I did
Wrote SQL (MySQL) queries to look at sales, margin, returns, and delivery by region and category
Cleaned data and calculated KPIs in Excel
Built an executive dashboard covering net sales, estimated contribution margin, return rate, and on-time delivery
